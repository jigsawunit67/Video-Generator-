Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Story Bot is a personal automation tool operated by its developer for the developer's own accounts. It has no public users and no sign-up.

What data is used

  • TikTok / YouTube authorization: when the developer connects their own account, the platform issues an access token. The token is stored only as an encrypted secret in the developer's own GitHub repository and is used solely to upload videos to that account.
  • Basic account information: the scope user.info.basic is used only to identify the connected account.
  • Public Reddit content: post titles and text of publicly available stories are read in order to create videos. Authors are credited in each video caption.
  • Text-to-speech: the story text is sent to an online text-to-speech service to generate the narration.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • We do not access private messages, contacts or any data beyond uploading videos to the connected account.

Retention and deletion

Generated videos are deleted after upload. The only stored record is a list of Reddit post IDs that were already used. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok or Google account settings, which immediately invalidates the stored token.
