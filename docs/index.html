Story Bot

Personal automation tool

Story Bot is a personal tool that creates short vertical videos from publicly available text stories on Reddit and publishes them to the developer's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

For every video it selects a popular story, generates narration with text-to-speech, adds animated captions, places the narration over a royalty-free or self-made background clip, and uploads the result through the official TikTok Content Posting API. Each caption credits the original author and subreddit.

The app only posts to the account of its developer, who authorizes it once. It does not offer public sign-up and does not process data of other users.

Questions: please open an issue in the project repository.
